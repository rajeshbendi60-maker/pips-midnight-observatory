📖 Bedtime Storybook

Pip and the Lost Little Star

Read a cozy illustrated bedtime story with Pip! Help a fallen little star find its way back home to the celestial nursery before sunrise.

Page 1 of 4

Chapter 1: A Glimmer in the Grass

Late at night, when all the nursery was asleep, Pip spotted a tiny golden twinkle behind the observatory shrubs. It was a fallen star named Spark, no bigger than a thimble, shivering softly in the midnight dew.

Chapter 2: The Moon Garden's Whisper

Pip scooped up Spark into the warm brass lantern and carried him to the Moon Garden. The night-blooming lunar lilies and sleepy moon-moths whispered softly: "Spark belongs to the Great Dipper! Follow the golden dream route up to Star Harbor."

Chapter 3: The Golden Route to Star Harbor

Unfurling the cloud-glider's starlight wings, Pip soared along the glowing golden dream delivery route. Gliding past Pillow Mountain and through lavender stardust clouds, Spark hummed with joy as his home constellation came into sight.

Chapter 4: Safe in the Constellation

With one last joyful hop, Spark clicked right back into his constellation spot, shining brighter than ever! The sky rained warm, golden sleep-dust over the observatory, and Pip glided safely home to tuck into bed for the sweetest dreams.

📖 Origin Story

How Pip Became a Dream Engineer

Scene 1

The First Dream

Pip wakes beneath a silver moon in a quiet nursery, not sure how long the nap lasted. Outside, the stars are in full shift — spinning their slow nighttime circles. Then a tiny light falls from the sky and lands, warm and trembling, in Pip's hands. It's a dream that has lost its way. Small. Glowing. Waiting. Pip holds it gently and thinks: someone is sleeping, and this is meant for them.

Scene 2

The Midnight Workshop

Deep in the nursery, Pip discovers a dusty old room. On the workbench: an old music box that hums a half-forgotten tune, a sleepy lantern whose glow changes colour when you whisper to it, and a star-chart map drawn entirely from constellations. Something stirs. Pip reaches for the music box and turns the tiny key. The melody that floats out is small, but the dreams it carries are enormous.

Scene 3

The Dream Engineer

By the time the morning star appears, Pip has a plan. The old nursery becomes the Midnight Observatory — open every night after bedtime for everyone who needs a little wonder. Pip hangs a sign on the door, adjusts the nightcap, and sets to work: Junior Dream Engineer, officially on duty. Lullabies composed. Lanterns lit. Dreams delivered. The night shift has never been so good.
